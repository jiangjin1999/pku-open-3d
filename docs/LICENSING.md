The surface atlas was generated procedurally; it does not contain reference photographs. The model's editable data and generating code are supplied so that the OSM-derived database can be accessed alongside its rendered outputs.

## Maps and references

OSM's open database license is distinct from its [tile service policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/), which restricts bulk downloading and offline tile packages. Esri's [content terms summary](https://goto.arcgis.com/termsofuse/viewsummary) restricts redistribution and self-hosting of its imagery. Attribution alone does not authorize either activity. Esri separately describes limited permission to derive and share vector data in [World Imagery permitted uses](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=8e90a00a0a6845a49262e0b756f57a10); that permission is subject to its stated conditions and does not authorize distributing the imagery itself.

Reference photographs and videos are excluded from Git and release assets. Source links document provenance; they do not grant permission to copy the linked material.

## Scope of this review

This is an asset and licensing review, not a legal clearance of every modeled building or artwork. China's [Copyright Law](https://www.ncac.gov.cn/xxfb/flfg/flfg_532/202103/t20210309_50530.html) protects architectural and artistic works. Its public-place exceptions do not establish a blanket permission for every form of downloadable 3D reproduction. The project makes no claim that public-source material is automatically free of third-party rights.

For a rights concern, open an [issue](https://github.com/sldyns/PKU-3D/issues) with the affected file or building, the original source and the claimed rights.
